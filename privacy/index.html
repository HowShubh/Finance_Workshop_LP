Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information K K Create Pvt Ltd (“KK Create”, “we”, “us”) collects when you visit finance.kkcreate.in or sign up for our workshops, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • What you give us at checkout: your name, email address and phone number, and any other details you enter in the checkout form.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by our checkout partner TagMango and payment gateway Razorpay. Your card, UPI or bank details go directly to them; we do not see or store them.
  • Usage information: pages you visit, the device and browser you use, and how you arrived (for example, the link or ad you clicked), collected through Google Analytics and Vercel Web Analytics.
  • Messages you send us: anything you write to support by email or WhatsApp.

2. How we use it

  • To give you access to the workshop, recordings, bonuses and certificate you have bought.
  • To send you joining links, reminders and updates by email, SMS or WhatsApp.
  • To answer your questions and give support.
  • To understand which pages and campaigns work, and to improve our content and site.
  • To tell you about other KK Create workshops and programmes. You can opt out at any time.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

3. Cookies and local storage

Our analytics tools use cookies or similar technology to measure visits. Our offer page also saves a small timer in your browser’s local storage so its countdown is not reset when you refresh. You can clear or block these in your browser settings; the site will still work.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run the workshop, and only as much as they need:

  • TagMango (checkout and the learning portal at learn.kkcreate.in) and Razorpay (payments).
  • Google Analytics and Vercel (site analytics and hosting).
  • WhatsApp, if you join our community group; other members can see your name and number there.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as you have access to what you bought, and afterwards only as long as we need it for legal, tax or accounting reasons.

6. Keeping it safe

We use reputable providers and limit access to your information to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

7. Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Stop marketing messages by using the unsubscribe link, replying STOP, or writing to us.
  • Leave the WhatsApp community at any time.

To do any of these, write to support@kkcreate.in from the email you signed up with.

8. Children

Our workshops are meant for people aged 18 and over, or younger learners with a parent’s or guardian’s consent.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
